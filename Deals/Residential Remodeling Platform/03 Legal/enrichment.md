## 4. Illinois State Records & Regulatory — FRAMEWORK CONFIRMED, ENTITY SEARCHES PENDING NAME

**Licensing reality (confirmed):** Illinois has **no state general-contractor license**. Only roofing (IDFPR) and plumbing (IDPH) are state-licensed trades. General remodelers are regulated locally — the Village of Bolingbrook (and most suburbs in the 25-mile radius) require **municipal contractor registration** per job/year.
- Verify: seller's contractor registration + permit history with Bolingbrook Building & Zoning, and registrations in the other suburbs where they work.
- Verify: any plumbing/electrical/roofing scope is performed by properly licensed subs — with a 1099 model this is the main compliance exposure.

**Home Repair and Remodeling Act (815 ILCS 513) — key compliance check:** contracts over $1,000 require a signed written contract AND delivery of the "Home Repair: Know Your Consumer Rights" pamphlet with a signed Consumer Rights Acknowledgment. Ask for sample signed contracts; non-compliance creates Consumer Fraud Act exposure that would transfer badly in an asset purchase and is a negotiating lever.

**Mechanics-lien exposure (770 ILCS 60):** with 5 subcontractors, confirm the seller collects **final lien waivers** on every completed job and inspect waiver files for the last 24 months of projects.
